# USDrop.ai — Product Strategy, Pricing & GTM (source of truth)

> Read this first. This defines WHAT usdrop.ai is, WHY we're building it, WHO it's for,
> HOW the money works, and the GTM. The portal (this repo) exists to *deliver and control*
> this program at scale. Status: v1 draft, being validated with the founder (Lakshay).

## 1. What it is (positioning)
**The done-for-you way for Indians to build a real US dropshipping business — guided end-to-end inside one portal.**
Not a SaaS tool. A **premium guided service** that takes a beginner from zero → live US Shopify store + US LLC (payments/bank) + China-warehouse fulfillment, with a human POC controlling their journey and software + cheap trained reps doing the heavy lifting.

## 2. Why (business rationale — this is the cash cow)
- **Service model, not product-seller.** We make money on the *program + LLC*, NOT on sourcing/GMV.
  - 100 clients/mo × ~₹1L profit = **₹1 cr/mo profit** at low risk.
  - To make that on sourcing you'd need ~₹30 cr GMV/mo → huge compliance, capital, returns, and risk.
  - Service = safer, faster, tested, no seller-level compliance. **Win-win** (client gets a real business; we get paid for expertise + done-for-you).
- **Controlled UX.** We own the whole experience, so we can make it feel worth a premium and cap scope. **Not result-guaranteed** — we do everything in our control; ads/marketplace results are the client's.
- **Premium pricing, not SaaS.** $20/mo adds nothing; $100/mo scares them off. People have money — we deliver clear value and command a premium one-time/program fee. Proven previously.

## 3. Audience
- **Primary (build for this):** Indians who have NOT done ecom yet, ~₹3–5L exposable budget, discovered via Mr. Suprans' reels (1M+ reach), trust that "Suprans does US dropshipping well," ready to hear the plan but lack subject depth.
- **Secondary:** existing dropshippers (nice-to-have; not the focus).
- **India-first**, then anyone.

## 4. The pipeline (funnel)
1. Reel → **usdrop.ai** → free signup (phone).
2. Instantly into **Free Learning** → builds trust, novelty, confidence, curiosity; Mr. Suprans shows how he does US dropshipping *using usdrop.ai for everything*.
3. Free-learning completion = qualified + warmed → **program sale** (mentorship/Elite).
4. Week ~2: awareness + **LLC upsell** (~80% convert) — the big cross-sell.
5. Guided journey (POC-controlled, scheduled chapter unlocks so they can't binge day-1 and leave) delivers: research/curation, AI tools, done-for-you design/dev (logos/banners/Shopify site — reps + AI), Shopify integration, fulfillment setup, LLC + bank + gateway.
6. **Defined closure/graduation** at end of term → scope limiting (stops perpetual support drain). Optional paid alumni/continuity.

## 5. Value stack (what justifies the AOV)
Free Learning (funnel) · Mentorship + proven framework + controlled curriculum · Winning-product research & curation · AI Studio (banner/brand-kit/logo/copy → later ad-creatives, website) · Done-for-you design/dev (human reps + AI) · Shopify integration (skip the learning curve) · **Fulfillment from our China warehouse (major confidence/value prop — NOT a profit center)** · **US LLC + EIN + US bank + Stripe/PayPal** · few live sessions · cheatsheets/resources. → Everything-in-one-portal, controlled + capped.

- Target: **AOV ₹1.25–1.75L**, ~65–70% margin (low COGS), 100–200 sales/mo → ₹1–3 cr rev, ~₹1–2 cr profit.

Just 3 clean offers: Mentorship / LLC / Elite. **Marketplaces are self-serve** — show the list + DIY method (in the Marketplaces section + the resources cheat-sheet); no paid "marketplace assist."   - **Blended AOV target ₹1.25–1.5L** (2-step ~₹1.09L + Elite mix). Margin ~65–70% (low COGS). 100–200 sales/mo → ₹1–3cr rev, ~₹1–2cr profit.
- **Meta ads = BUILD a full in-portal AI ad suite** (adcreative.ai-style) — headline feature so self-serve covers most; keeps us out of agency work; teach in curriculum; referral partner for anyone insisting on done-for-you.
- **How they buy:** in-portal Program offer page with BOTH Razorpay self-checkout (hot buyers) AND "Book a demo/call" → rep closes on demo → enroll. Sell mostly on demo for ₹1L+.

## 7. Scope-creep policy (protect the model)
- **Meta ads:** DO NOT become an agency (reps aren't experts; results take time + aren't in our control). → Teach it in curriculum + ship a **self-serve AI ad-creative suite** (empower them) + optional **one-time paid ad *setup*** (not ongoing mgmt) + **referral/partner a Meta agency** for those who want done-for-you.
- **Marketplace approval (Amazon/Walmart/TikTok Shop):** result-dependent → **paid add-on, best-effort, explicitly no guarantee**, or partner. Never free.
- **Fulfillment:** value-add only, no sourcing margin (avoids GMV risk).

## 8. Ops model
- 1 POC/ops-head per ~100 clients (software does most; POC = journey control + closure).
- 1–2 service reps (domain connect, logo, banner, website, design) — AI assists.
- Pre-recorded training, scheduled unlocks, compress the journey, hard closure.
- Sales: usdrop demo sells more than cold calls → build **demo + call-booking** system; senior sales reps later.

## 9. Add / remove (product roadmap tied to money)
- **ADD:** AI ad-creative suite (offsets "run my ads" pressure), AI website generator (automate done-for-you site), scheduled chapter unlocks + POC journey control, in-portal **program offer + Razorpay checkout + enrollment**, closure/graduation flow, paid add-ons (ad setup, marketplace assist).
- **KEEP:** LLC in-portal, fulfillment as value-add, research/curation, AI tools, Shopify integration.
- **DON'T:** ongoing ad management, free marketplace approval, sourcing-margin business.
